Our matcha

From Yame, Japan
to your cup.

Our matcha comes from Yame, Fukuoka, a region known for its tea-growing tradition. We bring together distinct expressions of Japanese green tea so you can discover the character that suits your own ritual.

Whether you reach for the gentle brightness of Hinata or the grounded depth of Shinrin, we hope each cup gives you a moment to slow down and enjoy.
