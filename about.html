What AIKŌ means

Inspired by 愛好.

AIKŌ was inspired by the Japanese word 愛好 (aikō) — expressing love, appreciation, and a deep fondness for something you enjoy.

To us, the word captures what we hope AIKŌ can be: a space for the things we genuinely love and appreciate, no matter how simple they may be.

A quiet morning. A bowl of carefully whisked matcha. A few minutes to slow down. A ritual we look forward to.

Our philosophy

It's about more than just matcha.

AIKŌ was never meant to be just about the drink. It's about the feeling behind it — taking a moment to slow down, appreciating the ritual, and finding joy in something as simple as a bowl of matcha.

Because loving something isn't always about grand gestures. Sometimes, it is simply taking the time to appreciate it.

Our matcha

From Yame, Japan
to your cup.

Our matcha comes from Yame, Fukuoka, a region known for its tea-growing tradition.

We bring together distinct expressions of Japanese matcha, each with its own character, so you can discover the one that becomes part of your everyday ritual.

Whether it's the gentle brightness of Hinata or the grounded depth of Shinrin, we hope every cup gives you something to look forward to.
